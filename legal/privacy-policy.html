  • Obliger is for adults aged 18 and over.
  • Your information is stored in the United States by the service providers listed in Section 3.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your display name, email address, password, and date of birth. Your password is stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we cannot see it. A trial account requires only a display name and date of birth.

Date of birth. We use the date of birth you enter only to confirm that you are at least 18. We keep a record that you met the age requirement, not your full date of birth.

Content you create. Obliger is a shared task tracker, so most of what we store is content you write, including:

  • Circle names, and the display name you use in each Circle
  • Task names, subjects, notes, due dates, and recurrence settings
  • A record of each time a task is marked complete: who completed it, when, and any note added
  • A record of task status changes: who marked a task in progress, and when

This content is visible to every member of the Circle it belongs to, along with the display names of all members. Do not add anything to a task or note that you would not want every member of that Circle to see.

Sensitive information you choose to add. We do not ask for health, religious, or other sensitive information. Some Circles, such as families coordinating care, may choose to add details like medication times or appointments to tasks or notes. If you add this kind of information, we store it only to show it to the members of that Circle, and we do not use it for any other purpose. Please add only what you are comfortable sharing with every member of the Circle.

Reminder settings. To send reminders at the right local time, we store your device’s time zone, the hour you choose to receive reminders, and a push notification token issued by Expo, which identifies your device to the push delivery service.

Crash and error data. When the app encounters an error, our crash-reporting provider, Sentry, receives a report containing your device model, operating system version, app version, and technical details about the error, including a short trail of recent app activity. We do not link these reports to your account, and we take steps to redact tokens, identifiers, and other sensitive values before they are sent. Reports are retained for 30 days and then deleted.

Messages you send us. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the content of your message.

Connection data. When your device connects to our servers, our hosting and authentication provider processes your IP address and similar connection information to deliver the Services and protect them from abuse. When you visit our website, our website host (GitHub) processes your IP address and similar connection information to deliver the site.

What we do not collect. Obliger asks only for permission to send push notifications. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use analytics or tracking software.

Information about other people. If you add information about someone else, for example a family member’s name in a task, please make sure you are entitled to share it.

5. How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. In general, we keep your account information and content for as long as you have an account, subject to the rules below.

Deleting your account. You can delete your account at any time in Settings. If you own a Circle that has other members, you must remove those members or transfer ownership first. Deleting your account permanently removes your account, any Circles you solely own and the tasks in them, your completion history, your notification settings, and your push tokens. This cannot be undone. Records of your actions on tasks in Circles owned by other people, such as completing a task or changing its status, remain in that Circle’s history under the display name you used in that Circle. Tasks you created in a Circle you do not own are transferred to the owner of that Circle, along with their full history, including entries logged by you and by other members.

Deleting a Circle. A deleted Circle is hidden from all members immediately and stops appearing in reminders. Its contents are kept for a recovery period of 30 days so that a deletion made in error can be reversed, and are permanently deleted by an automated process shortly after that period ends. During the recovery period, no member can access the contents through the app.

Trial accounts. A trial account that is not converted to a full account within 30 days is permanently deleted with all its data. If you cancel a trial in Settings, the deletion happens immediately. If you convert a trial to a full account, your data is kept as part of that account.

Other information.

  • Crash and error data: kept by Sentry for up to 30 days
  • Records of account emails: kept by Resend for up to 30 days
  • Messages you send us: kept until your request is resolved, then archived permanently

We do not keep backups of your information.

If we need to keep information longer to comply with the law, resolve a dispute, or enforce our terms, we will keep only what is needed and isolate it from other processing until it can be deleted.

If you are in the EU, EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you have the right to access your personal information; correct it; erase it; restrict our processing of it; receive a copy of the information you gave us in a portable format; object to processing based on our legitimate interests; and withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing that took place before. How to make a request. Use our request page or email contact@obligerapp.com. We will need to verify your identity, usually by confirming access to the email address on your account, and we will use the information you give us for verification only for that purpose.

10. Children

Obliger is for adults aged 18 and over, and children may not create accounts. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from anyone under 18. An adult may add limited information about a child to a task, such as a first name or nickname in a chore. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it. If you believe this has happened, contact us at contact@obligerapp.com.
